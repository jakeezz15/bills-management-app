# Privacy Policy — On Hand

**Last updated:** 9 September 2026  
**Developer:** Jake Durante  
**App:** On Hand (`com.durantetechsolutions.onhand`)  
**Store listing title:** On Hand: What's left?

This policy is for the mobile app On Hand. It is written for a public store listing. Host this page at a stable URL (GitHub, a personal site, or similar) and paste that URL into Play Console / App Store Connect.

## Summary

On Hand is a personal leftover tracker. **Your financial entries stay on your device.** We do not create an account for you, we do not connect to your bank, and we do not operate a server that receives your income, spending, bills, savings, or debts.

## What the app stores

On your phone only (using on-device storage):

- Income, everyday spending, bills, savings goals, debts, and payment / contribution history you enter
- Display currency and whether due-day reminders are on
- Simple UI preferences (for example, which Plans or Activity section you last opened)

We do not collect this information on our servers because there is no app backend.

## What we do not collect

- Name, email, or account credentials (the app has no sign-in)
- Bank, card, or brokerage credentials
- Location, contacts, or photos (except a backup file you explicitly pick)
- Analytics, advertising IDs, or crash reports sent to us by this app as of this policy date

If a store or OS shows a system dialog (notifications, file picker), that is the operating system helping you use a feature you turned on.

## Notifications

Optional **due-day reminders** are scheduled **locally** on your device (for example, 9:00 AM on a bill or debt due day). They are not push messages from our servers. You can turn them off in Settings.

## Backups

**Export backup** writes a JSON file of your finance data that you can save or share yourself (email, Drive, Files, etc.).  
**Import backup** replaces the data on this device with a file you choose.

If you share that file, whoever you send it to can read it. We never receive it unless you send it to us for support.

## Deleting your data

- Settings → **Reset all data** clears finance entries on this device  
- Uninstalling the app removes on-device app data (unless your OS backs up app storage)

There is no cloud copy for us to delete.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## Not financial advice

Figures in the app are what you typed. The app does not provide banking, investment, tax, or legal advice.

## Changes

If we add an account, cloud sync, or any service that receives your data, we will update this policy and the store listing before that version ships.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email listed on the app’s store page.
